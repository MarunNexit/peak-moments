# Privacy Policy **Last updated: 23 November 2026** ## 1. Introduction Peak Moments ("we", "our", or "the Application") is a video clipping application designed to help users create short-form videos from longer videos and livestreams. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use the Application and how that information is handled. ## 2. Information We May Process Depending on the features you use, the Application may process: ### Video and Media Data - Video URLs provided by users. - Video and audio content processed to generate clips. - Generated video clips and subtitles. - Video titles and related metadata. ### TikTok Account Information When you connect your TikTok account, the Application may receive information through TikTok's official APIs, including: - Basic TikTok account information. - Authorization tokens required to access approved TikTok features. - Information necessary to publish videos to your TikTok account. The exact information depends on the permissions you authorize. ### Application Configuration The Application may process settings such as: - AI provider configuration. - Video processing preferences. - API credentials configured by the user. ## 3. How Information Is Used Information may be processed for the following purposes: - Generating short video clips. - Transcribing audio and creating subtitles. - Identifying interesting moments using AI. - Managing video exports. - Connecting authorized TikTok accounts. - Publishing videos when explicitly requested by the user. - Maintaining and troubleshooting the Application. ## 4. Local Processing and Third-Party Services Peak Moments is designed to run locally on the user's computer. Video files, generated clips, configuration files, and other application data may be stored locally, depending on the user's configuration. Some features may communicate with external services, including: - TikTok APIs for account authorization and video publishing. - YouTube and Twitch for accessing supported content. - AI providers for video, audio, or text processing. - Cloudflare Tunnel or similar services for secure connectivity. When external AI providers or third-party services are used, relevant data may be transmitted to those providers for processing. Their handling of information is governed by their own privacy policies and terms. ## 5. TikTok Integration If you choose to connect your TikTok account: - Authorization is performed through TikTok's official authorization process. - The Application requests only the permissions required for the enabled features. - Authorization tokens are used to provide the functionality you requested. - Videos are submitted for publication only when you initiate the publishing process. TikTok processes information according to its own Privacy Policy. You can revoke the Application's access through your TikTok account settings. ## 6. Data Storage and Retention Peak Moments is designed for local use. Application data may be stored on your device, including generated clips, settings, and authorization information. The Application does not operate a centralized user account database as part of its standard local configuration. Data stored by third-party providers is subject to their respective retention policies. You can delete locally stored application files and revoke third-party authorizations where applicable. ## 7. Data Sharing Peak Moments does not sell users' personal information. Information may be transmitted to third-party services when necessary to provide features explicitly requested by the user. We do not intentionally share personal information with third parties for their independent advertising purposes. ## 8. Data Security Users are responsible for protecting their devices, local files, API keys, and application credentials. We recommend keeping API credentials private and using secure devices and networks. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. ## 9. Children's Privacy Peak Moments is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. ## 10. Your Privacy Rights Depending on your location and applicable laws, you may have rights to: - Request access to personal information. - Request correction or deletion of personal information. - Object to certain processing activities. - Withdraw authorization for connected third-party services. For information about data processed by TikTok or other third-party providers, please consult their respective privacy policies. ## 11.
